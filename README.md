# ✦ STARFALL PACT ✦

A shared star-chart card duel for the make.fun best card game bounty. You and the
rival stargazer ASTRAEA place stars on the **same 3×3 star chart**. Complete a row
(⚔ 8 damage), a column (✚ heal 6) or a diagonal (🃏 draw 3) — same-suit lines add
+4. Block her constellations by taking the cell she is eyeing (she telegraphs it a
full turn ahead). Cards cost 🔥 Ember and deal their star value in damage. First to
0 Life wins. 30 Life each side, draw 2 per turn, four suits told apart by colour
and shape: Ember 🔥, Tide 💧, Stone ⛰️, Gale 🌀.

Play it: open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser. Keys: 1–7 play a card,
Space ends turn, R restarts, M mutes.
